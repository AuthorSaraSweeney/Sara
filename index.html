Welcome, reader

Come,
sit a while.

Reading Room Press is an independent literary press rooted in the belief that stories are sacred — that the right book at the right moment can change the very shape of a life.

We are small by design and intentional by nature. Every title we bring into the world is chosen with care, published with devotion, and sent forth like a seed into fertile ground.

This is our reading room. The wallpaper is botanical, the light is amber, there is always something steeping on the stove, and the shelves go further than they should. Come in.

We believe in the strange alchemy that happens between a reader and a page — in the book that finds you exactly when you need it. We are here to make those books, and to send them out into the world like small, lit lanterns.

The Author

Sara
Sweeney

Sara Sweeney writes stories that exist in the spaces between — between past and present, between love and loss, between the world as it is and the world as it should be.

She is drawn to the doors that shouldn't open, the rooms that hold more than they should, and the women who refuse to leave quietly. Her prose lives in the territory where the historical and the supernatural press against each other until something breaks through.

She is the founder of Reading Room Press, built on the conviction that independent publishing is an act of devotion. The Year That Wasn't Hers is her debut novel.
