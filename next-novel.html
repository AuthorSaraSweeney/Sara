Inside Sunday Best

She already knows what it costs to start over.

Desire comes easily.
Trust is another matter.

Mara Hayes spent ten years in a marriage where money, choices, and even her ambitions were subject to someone else’s approval. Two years after leaving, she has a place of her own and a food truck she runs with her best friend, Georgia. The hours are brutal and the margins are thin. But the life is one she chose.

Alix Delorenzi has built a successful development company and a reputation for getting things done. He also serves as underboss to his brother’s criminal organization. Money and influence have always given him options most people never have. He’s used to deciding which one to take.

At first, he notices the way Mara commands her kitchen. She notices how little he needs to say to make other people listen. Neither expects a single transaction to keep bringing them back into each other’s lives.

Then anonymous threats begin reaching Mara. Alix has money, security, and connections she doesn’t. What he lacks is the habit of asking before he uses them. For a woman who has already lived with control disguised as care, his help presents a problem of its own.

The attraction between them is immediate. Building a relationship Mara can choose freely, while Alix’s world closes around them, will demand more than either knows how to give.

Mara learns that accepting his protection may be just as risky as refusing it.

Beyond the first impression

Meet Mara & Alix

32 · Chef · Starting over

Mara Hayes

Sharp-tongued, observant, and warm with the people she lets close. Mara shows affection by feeding people and remembering what they like. She saves every receipt, hates financial surprises, and will work herself into the ground before admitting she needs help.

35 · Developer · Underboss

Alix Delorenzi

Tailored suits, quiet authority, and violence kept under tight control. Alix’s niece and nephew know a patient, playful uncle. The people who do business with his family know someone very different. Both sides of him are real.

The life worth protecting

Sunday Best belongs to two women.

Georgia Price helped Mara turn the truck from a dream into a business. They are equal partners, with Mara running the kitchen and Georgia handling customers, schedules, and the work that keeps them moving. The danger that follows Mara reaches Georgia, too. Their friendship and the business they built remain central to the story.

A new interconnected series

City of Favors

The families, the people they love, and the obligations between them.

In Glennon, the Delorenzis’ influence runs through downtown property, development, and hospitality. Along the working riverfront, the Kellehers have their own history and claim to power. As old industrial land becomes valuable again, the boundaries between them are harder to keep.

City of Favors follows the romances woven through these families and their wider circle of friends, allies, and rivals. Some people inherit the obligations. Others become entangled with them through the people they love.

Sunday Best begins with Mara and Alix. Future books will follow other couples into different corners of this connected world.

The reading experience

Danger, desire, and difficult choices.

Attraction before trust

The chemistry arrives before either of them is ready for it. Emotional trust develops through friction, vulnerability, and the choices they make when it would be easier to take control.

A heroine with a life to keep

Mara has a home, a livelihood, and a friendship that matter before Alix enters the picture. Falling in love means finding room for him without surrendering those parts of herself.

Family ties with consequences

Business, loyalty, and personal history reach into every relationship. The criminal intrigue puts pressure on a romance that remains at the center of the book.

For adult readers. The story includes explicit intimacy, violence, threats, and a history of financial and emotional abuse.
