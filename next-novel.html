Mara & Alix

One job.
A dangerous connection.

Attraction is the easy part.
Trust will have to be earned.

Mara Hayes has fought too hard for her independence to hand it over to another man. Her food truck, Sunday Best, is her livelihood, her second chance, and the one place where she makes the rules.

A last-minute catering job brings her into the orbit of Alix Delorenzi. He has money, influence, and a family name that makes people think twice. He’s also far too accustomed to solving other people’s problems without asking.

Then a shooting turns Sunday Best into a getaway vehicle, and keeping her distance becomes a lot more complicated.

Alix knows how to protect what matters to him. Mara knows the cost of letting someone else decide what’s best for her. With danger closing in and attraction becoming harder to ignore, they’ll have to learn the difference between offering protection and taking control.

He can offer her protection.
She’ll decide what to accept.
