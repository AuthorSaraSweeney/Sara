# Reading Room Press website expansion

Status: planning draft on the `reading-room-press-expansion` branch. Do not merge until pages are reviewed.

## Goal
Expand the existing Reading Room Press publishing website into a home for the imprint, author services, literary research, curated secondhand books, and future downloadable resources. Preserve the existing visual identity and published author/book information.

## Proposed navigation
- Home
- Books
- The Author's Desk
- The Research Room
- The Secondhand Shelf
- Resources (coming later)
- Contact

## First launch: Author's Desk
**Author Launch Essentials**: Pilot price $149. Book description, retailer metadata suggestions, and a tailored launch checklist. One consolidated revision. Scope and turnaround must be agreed before payment.

**Publishing Setup Consultation**: Pilot price $99. A 60-minute consultation covering KDP/IngramSpark setup and a follow-up checklist. The client keeps control of their publishing accounts.

## First launch: Research Room
**Historical Fiction Research Brief**: Pilot price $249. One focused historical question, a 3–5 page brief, cited sources and uncertainty notes. Target 8–12 sources where available. Confirm scope and feasibility before accepting payment.

## Secondhand Shelf
Curated used mystery and crime paperbacks. Start with author/series bundles and local pickup. Show actual inventory and condition only after books are cataloged. Avoid inventing stock or prices.

## Future Resources
Original research and author templates, released after testing demand.

## Contact and checkout
Start with a simple inquiry link to the existing business contact. Do not collect sensitive payment information on a static form. Confirm payment provider and policies before adding checkout links.

## Technical implementation checklist
1. Identify actual published page(s), stylesheets, asset directories, and GitHub Pages deployment settings.
2. Confirm whether root `index.html` is intentionally empty and where the live site's source lives.
3. Match current fonts, colors, navigation, mobile layout, and accessibility conventions.
4. Build pages on this branch and review them before merging into `main`.
5. Test navigation, mobile layout, keyboard accessibility, and external links.
6. Update any outdated release or preorder copy only after verifying the source page.

## Business launch guardrails
No debt or new paid software required. Pilot pricing is provisional. Track hours, revenue, expenses, and net proceeds. Separate one-time used-book proceeds from recurring service revenue.
