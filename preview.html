Ask about a research brief

What might you ask?

What would a librarian's daily work have looked like in 1930s Wales? How did people travel between towns? What language would a character have used for an object or occupation?

Research briefs distinguish supported findings from plausible interpretation and unanswered questions.

Research with care

Our approach values clear citations, context, and transparency about uncertainty. Research materials are intended to inform your creative work and do not replace legal, medical, or other regulated professional advice.
