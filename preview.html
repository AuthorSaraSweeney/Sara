Tailored research support

01

Custom Research Portfolio

Quoted by project

An organized collection of sources, findings, and practical notes built around an agreed part of your project. A setting, a character’s profession, a group of connected questions, or selected passages can define the scope.

Your quote specifies the topics or passages, deliverables, research-time limit, fee, and delivery date. One round of clarification is included. New questions or further research are agreed separately.

Discuss your project

What would help your writing?

The work sets the format.

An annotated source guide when you need a stronger foundation.

Organized notes or a timeline when you need context.

An evidence-backed fact-check when you need to verify details.

We agree on the format before booking. Length and source count follow the research, with no arbitrary page target.

Deliverable: Annotated source guide

02

Historical
context

Findings organized around related needs such as setting, work, travel, or daily life. The agreed output might be topic notes, a timeline, or a research summary, with citations and unresolved questions clearly marked.

Deliverable: Organized research notes

03

Manuscript
fact-checking

A review of agreed passages or factual claims, with source-backed notes identifying what is supported, what conflicts with the evidence, and what remains uncertain. The amount of material is set before booking.

Deliverable: Annotated review notes

Choose one service or combine them within an agreed scope. A focused check can stay focused. A broader project can bring several related research needs together.
