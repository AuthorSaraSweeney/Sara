Meet the person behind the press

A librarian’s eye.
An author’s understanding.

Reading Room Press brings together the work of Sara Mautino, MSLS, an academic librarian with nearly 20 years of research experience, and the fiction she publishes as Sara Sweeney.

Through her research and author services, Sara helps writers find reliable information and navigate the practical details of independent publishing. As an author herself, she understands the care, uncertainty, and expense involved in bringing a book into the world.

A small press with room for more

Original fiction, practical support for fellow writers, and well-loved books finding new readers. Each has a place at Reading Room Press.

Three ways to use Research Assist

Choose the help that’s most useful to you.

Find the right sources

A targeted source search

“I need credible sources on the daily work of a British housemaid in the 1920s. Where should I start?”

Receive a carefully selected source guide with references, links or location information, and brief notes explaining what each source offers. Where identifiable, I’ll point you toward particularly useful chapters or sections.

Best when you want to do the reading yourself and need a good place to begin.

Check the details

A scene-detail check

“Here’s a short scene. Can you check these transportation details and flag anything I should investigate?”

Receive comments on the specific details we agree to review, with supporting sources and clear notes about conflicting or uncertain information. This covers selected factual details in a short excerpt.

Best when you’ve written something and want to know whether particular details hold up.

Get a research answer

A focused research inquiry

“I’m struggling to work out how my character would accomplish this particular task in this place and year.”

Receive a concise explanation of what I found, the evidence behind it, and what the findings mean for your scene or character.

Best when you need someone to investigate a contained problem and explain the results.

Each $50 booking covers one agreed task from the options above. A brief scope check helps us determine whether your request fits or would benefit from a larger package.
